### 🚀 About Me

I'm an **Aerospace Engineering graduate from IOE Pulchowk Campus, Tribhuvan University, Nepal**, with a hands-on core in propulsion and fabrication. I design, machine, and test rocket engines and airframes — not just simulate them.

- 🔭 Led propulsion & fabrication for **Project TRISHUL**, an L-class sounding rocket that set a **2.62 km national altitude record** in Nepal
- 🛠️ Designed, machined, and static-fired **LUME-X1**, a pressure-fed liquid rocket engine (triplet injector, ethanol/GOX, 121 N achieved vs. 130 N predicted)
- 🧪 Completed a research internship at **IIT Bombay's Propellant Research Laboratory** (AP/HTPB propellant testing, Crawford Bomb, hybrid motor hot fire, laser ignition)
- 🤖 Represented **Nepal at ABU Robocon 2023** (Phnom Penh) — quarter-finalist, Mabuchi Motor Award for compact design
- ✈️ Contributed to a High-Altitude Pseudo-Satellite (HAPS) aircraft preliminary design project ("Phoenix") using X-Plane, XFLR5, and SUAVE
- ⚙️ Currently exploring a CAD/CNC-driven venture modernizing a family goldsmithing business
