### About

I build propulsion hardware from first principles through to a live static fire. Aerospace Engineering graduate of IOE Pulchowk Campus, Tribhuvan University, with a track record that runs from CEA performance modeling and CATIA design, into the machine shop, and out onto the test stand. I led propulsion and fabrication on a national-record sounding rocket, machined and fired a liquid engine of my own design, and represented Nepal at an international robotics competition. The common thread is ownership of hardware end to end — not just the analysis.

### Selected project

**LUME-X1 — Pressure-Fed Liquid Rocket Engine**

Designed, machined, and static-fired independently, start to finish.

| | |
|---|---|
| Propellants | Ethanol / GOX |
| Injector | Triplet impinging |
| Predicted thrust | 130 N (NASA CEA) |
| Achieved thrust | 121 N, first static fire |

The full pipeline: performance modeling in CEA, injector and chamber design in CATIA V5, in-house machining, then instrumented static fire. The 7% delta between predicted and achieved thrust came down mostly to injector discharge coefficient — logged for the next revision.

*Also on record: **Project TRISHUL**, an L-class sounding rocket that reached 2.62 km — Nepal's national altitude record — where I led propulsion and fabrication; and **ABU Robocon 2023**, where I served as Design & Fabrication Head for Nepal's national team, reaching the quarter-finals and winning the Mabuchi Motor Award for compact design.*
