## 👋 About

Aerospace engineer built around one principle: **don't just simulate it — build it, machine it, and static-fire it.** I graduated from the **Institute of Engineering, Pulchowk Campus, Tribhuvan University**, and spent my degree in the workshop as much as the classroom — leading propulsion and fabrication on liquid and solid rocket systems, a national-record-setting sounding rocket, and an award-winning robotics platform at an international competition.

My core strength sits at the intersection of **propellant chemistry, CAD/CAE design, and hands-on fabrication** — I take a concept from a CEA/RPA performance model through CATIA design, CNC/lathe machining, and into a live static-fire test.

## 🔬 Current Research

**Propellant Research Laboratory, IIT Bombay** — *Aircraft Propulsion Laboratory Internship*
Collaborative experimental research on solid propellant characterization and hybrid propulsion:
- AP/HTPB composite propellant testing and burn-rate characterization
- Crawford Bomb closed-vessel combustion testing
- Hybrid rocket motor hot-fire testing
- Laser ignition systems and Electrically Controlled Solid Propellant (ECSP) evaluation

**Parallel track:** exploring a CAD/CNC-driven modernization of a multi-generation family goldsmithing business — applying precision engineering tooling outside aerospace.

### 🔥 LUME-X1 — Liquid Rocket Engine
Pressure-fed liquid engine, designed and static-fired solo through the full pipeline.
- **Propellants:** Ethanol / GOX
- **Injector:** Triplet impinging design
- **Result:** 121 N achieved vs. 130 N predicted on first static fire
- Full CEA performance modeling → CATIA design → in-house machining

### 🚀 Project TRISHUL — Sounding Rocket
L-class sounding rocket, propulsion & fabrication lead.
- **Achievement:** 2.62 km — **national altitude record, Nepal**
- Developed in partnership with Worldlink Communications
- Full-stack ownership: motor, airframe fabrication, static fire
